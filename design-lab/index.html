Good to know

Questions, answered.

Are practice scores official PTE scores?

No. Results in this mock represent practice estimates and are not affiliated with or issued by Pearson.

Can I practise one skill at a time?

Yes. Use the practice screen to focus your session on a skill or task type.

Do I need to make an account to explore?

This design prototype is open to explore. The controls demonstrate the intended product flow only.
